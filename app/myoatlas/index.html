How to use MyoAtlas

  • Explore – drag to spin the body, tap a muscle for its origin, insertion, nerve supply and action.
  • Peel layers – superficial muscles hide deeper ones. Use Hide on a selected muscle, the eye icon in the list, or Solo a region.
  • Skeleton – Display ▸ Skeleton shows bones as a ghost or solid for context.
  • Quiz – Locate a named muscle, name a highlighted one, or recall its nerve supply. Filter by region.

Changes made — Only the muscular and skeletal systems were used. Fasciae, bursae and tendon sheaths were removed; tendons were attached to their muscles. The reduced-detail meshes were split per structure, vertices welded and quantised, and the result repacked into compact data files. Muscles were regrouped by region and heads/parts merged under one muscle. Normals are recomputed in the browser.
