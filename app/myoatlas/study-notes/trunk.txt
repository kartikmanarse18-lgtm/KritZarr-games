## BACK — SUPERFICIAL (extrinsic)
trapezius | Superior nuchal line, external occipital protuberance, ligamentum nuchae, spinous processes C7–T12 | Lateral third of clavicle, acromion and spine of scapula | Accessory n. (CN XI) motor; C3–C4 proprioception | Elevates, retracts, depresses and rotates the scapula; extends the neck | CN XI injury (e.g. during lymph-node biopsy in the posterior triangle): drooping shoulder, weak shrug.
part:descending part of trapezius | Superior nuchal line, external occipital protuberance, ligamentum nuchae | Lateral third of clavicle | Elevates the scapula (shrugging)
part:transverse part of trapezius | Spinous processes of C7–T3 | Acromion and upper border of scapular spine | Retracts the scapula
part:ascending part of trapezius | Spinous processes of T4–T12 | Tubercle at the root of the scapular spine | Depresses the scapula
latissimus dorsi | Spinous processes T7–L5 via thoracolumbar fascia, posterior iliac crest, lower 3–4 ribs, inferior angle of scapula | Floor of the intertubercular groove of the humerus | Thoracodorsal n. (C6–C8) | Extends, adducts and medially rotates the arm (climbing, rowing, crutch-walking) | Forms the posterior axillary fold with teres major.
levator scapulae | Transverse processes of C1–C4 | Superior angle and upper medial border of the scapula | Dorsal scapular n. (C5) and cervical nn. C3–C4 | Elevates the scapula; rotates the glenoid cavity inferiorly
rhomboid major | Spinous processes of T2–T5 | Medial border of scapula, below the spine | Dorsal scapular n. (C4–C5) | Retracts the scapula; rotates the glenoid inferiorly; holds the scapula to the thorax
rhomboid minor | Ligamentum nuchae and spinous processes of C7–T1 | Medial border of scapula at the root of the spine | Dorsal scapular n. (C4–C5) | Retracts the scapula
serratus posterior superior | Ligamentum nuchae, spinous processes C7–T3 | Upper borders of ribs 2–5 | Intercostal nn. (T1–T4) | Elevates the ribs (proprioceptive in inspiration)
serratus posterior inferior | Spinous processes of T11–L2 | Inferior borders of ribs 9–12 | Intercostal nn. (T9–T12) | Depresses the lower ribs (proprioceptive in expiration)

## BACK — DEEP (intrinsic, all supplied by dorsal rami of spinal nerves)

longissimus thoracis | Common erector spinae origin: sacrum, iliac crest, lumbar spinous processes | Ribs 4–12 and transverse processes of thoracic and lumbar vertebrae | Dorsal rami of spinal nn. | Extends and laterally flexes the vertebral column | The intermediate (largest) column of the erector spinae.

multifidus lumborum | Sacrum, posterior superior iliac spine, mamillary processes of lumbar vertebrae | Spinous processes 2–4 segments above | Dorsal rami of spinal nn. | Stabilises the vertebral column; extends and rotates to the opposite side | Key stabiliser of the lumbar spine; atrophies in chronic low back pain.

ventral parts of lateral intertransversarii lumborum | Transverse processes of lumbar vertebrae (ventral part) | Transverse process of the vertebra above | Ventral rami of lumbar nn. | Lateral flexion of the lumbar spine

## THORAX
pectoralis major | Clavicular head: medial half of the clavicle. Sternocostal head: sternum and upper 6 costal cartilages. Abdominal part: external oblique aponeurosis | Lateral lip of the intertubercular groove of the humerus | Lateral and medial pectoral nn. (C5–T1) | Adducts and medially rotates the arm; clavicular head flexes the arm; sternocostal head extends it from a flexed position | Forms the anterior axillary fold. Poland syndrome: congenital absence.
part:clavicular head of pectoralis major | Medial half of the clavicle | Lateral lip of intertubercular groove | Lateral pectoral n. (C5–C6). Flexes the arm
part:sternocostal head of pectoralis major | Anterior sternum, costal cartilages 1–6 | Lateral lip of intertubercular groove | Medial and lateral pectoral nn. (C6–T1). Extends the flexed arm
part:abdominal part of pectoralis major | Aponeurosis of external abdominal oblique | Lateral lip of intertubercular groove | Medial pectoral n. Part of the lowest fibres
pectoralis minor | Ribs 3–5 near the costal cartilages | Coracoid process of scapula | Medial pectoral n. (C8–T1) | Protracts and depresses the scapula; elevates ribs when the scapula is fixed | Landmark dividing the axillary artery into three parts.
subclavius | First rib and its costal cartilage | Subclavian groove on the inferior clavicle | Nerve to subclavius (C5–C6) | Depresses the clavicle; stabilises the sternoclavicular joint
serratus anterior | External surfaces of ribs 1–8 (9) | Costal surface of the medial border of the scapula | Long thoracic n. (C5–C7) | Protracts and upward-rotates the scapula; holds it against the ribcage (punching) | Long thoracic nerve injury (e.g. mastectomy) causes a winged scapula.
external intercostal | Inferior border of the rib above | Superior border of the rib below | Intercostal nn. (T1–T11) | Elevate the ribs in inspiration | Fibres run "hands in pockets" (down and forward). Neurovascular bundle runs in the costal groove (VAN: vein, artery, nerve, top to bottom).
internal intercostal | Costal groove of the rib above | Superior border of the rib below | Intercostal nn. (T1–T11) | Depress the ribs in forced expiration; interosseous part | Fibres run perpendicular to the external layer.
innermost intercostal | Costal groove of the rib above | Superior border of the rib below | Intercostal nn. (T1–T11) | Assists internal intercostals | Neurovascular bundle lies between internal and innermost layers.
transversus thoracis | Posterior surface of lower sternum and xiphoid process | Inner surfaces of costal cartilages 2–6 | Intercostal nn. (T2–T6) | Weakly depresses the ribs (expiration)
levatores breves costarum | Transverse processes of C7–T11 | Rib immediately below, near the tubercle | Dorsal rami of C8–T11 | Elevate the ribs; assist extension and rotation of the spine
levatores longi costarum | Transverse processes of C7–T11 | Rib two below, near the tubercle | Dorsal rami of C8–T11 | Elevate the ribs; assist extension and rotation
diaphragm | Sternal: xiphoid process. Costal: lower 6 ribs. Lumbar: crura (L1–L3) and arcuate ligaments | Central tendon | Phrenic n. (C3–C5); peripheral sensory: intercostal nn. | Main muscle of inspiration: contracts, flattens and increases thoracic volume | "C3, 4, 5 keeps the diaphragm alive." Aortic hiatus T12, oesophageal hiatus T10, caval opening T8.

## ABDOMEN
rectus abdominis | Pubic symphysis and pubic crest | Xiphoid process and costal cartilages 5–7 | Ventral rami of T7–T12 (intercostal and subcostal nn.) | Flexes the trunk; compresses the abdomen; stabilises the pelvis | Enclosed in the rectus sheath; the three tendinous intersections give the "six-pack".
external abdominal oblique | External surfaces of ribs 5–12 | Linea alba, pubic tubercle and anterior half of the iliac crest | Ventral rami of T7–T12 (intercostal, subcostal, iliohypogastric, ilioinguinal) | Compresses the abdomen; flexes and rotates the trunk to the opposite side | Its aponeurosis forms the inguinal ligament; fibres run "hands in pockets".
internal abdominal oblique | Thoracolumbar fascia, anterior 2/3 of iliac crest, lateral 2/3 of inguinal ligament | Inferior borders of ribs 10–12, linea alba and pubic crest (via conjoint tendon) | Ventral rami of T7–L1 | Compresses the abdomen; rotates the trunk to the same side | Gives rise to the cremaster muscle.
transversus abdominis | Costal cartilages 7–12, thoracolumbar fascia, iliac crest, lateral 1/3 of inguinal ligament | Linea alba and pubic crest (via conjoint tendon) | Ventral rami of T7–L1 | Compresses the abdominal contents; supports the abdominal wall | The deepest flat muscle; a key "core" stabiliser.
pyramidalis | Pubic crest and symphysis | Linea alba | Subcostal n. (T12) | Tenses the linea alba | Absent in about 20% of people.
quadratus lumborum | Iliac crest and iliolumbar ligament | 12th rib and transverse processes of L1–L4 | Ventral rami of T12–L4 | Laterally flexes the trunk; fixes the 12th rib during inspiration | Posterior abdominal wall; kidneys lie in front of it.

## PELVIC FLOOR
coccygeus | Ischial spine and sacrospinous ligament | Lateral margin of coccyx and lowest sacrum | Ventral rami of S3–S4 | Supports the pelvic viscera; flexes the coccyx
iliococcygeus | Tendinous arch of levator ani and ischial spine | Anococcygeal raphe and coccyx | Nerve to levator ani (S3–S4); perineal branch of S4 | Supports the pelvic viscera
pubococcygeus | Posterior surface of the body of pubis | Anococcygeal raphe and coccyx | Nerve to levator ani (S3–S4) | Supports pelvic viscera; resists intra-abdominal pressure | Injured in vaginal delivery, leading to prolapse and incontinence.
pubo-analis | Body of the pubis | Forms a sling around the anorectal junction | Nerve to levator ani (S3–S4) | Maintains the anorectal angle (faecal continence) | Corresponds to puborectalis.
external anal sphincter | Perineal body and anococcygeal body | Encircles the anal canal | Inferior rectal n. (S2–S4; branch of pudendal n.) | Voluntary closure of the anal canal
