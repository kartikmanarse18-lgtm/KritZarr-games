## EYE
superior rectus | Common tendinous ring (annulus of Zinn) | Superior sclera, behind the corneoscleral limbus | Oculomotor n. (CN III), superior division | Elevates the eye; also adducts and intorts | Mnemonic for eye-muscle nerves: LR6 SO4 AO3 — lateral rectus CN VI, superior oblique CN IV, all others CN III.
inferior rectus | Common tendinous ring | Inferior sclera | Oculomotor n. (CN III), inferior division | Depresses the eye; also adducts and extorts
medial rectus | Common tendinous ring | Medial sclera | Oculomotor n. (CN III), inferior division | Adducts the eye | Largest and strongest extra-ocular muscle.
lateral rectus | Common tendinous ring | Lateral sclera | Abducens n. (CN VI) | Abducts the eye | CN VI palsy: cannot abduct, medial squint (esotropia).
superior oblique | Body of sphenoid above the optic canal; tendon loops through the trochlea | Posterolateral sclera (superior aspect) | Trochlear n. (CN IV) | Intorts, depresses (esp. when adducted) and abducts the eye | CN IV palsy: vertical diplopia worse on looking down (e.g. stairs).
inferior oblique | Orbital surface of maxilla (anteromedial floor of orbit) | Posterolateral sclera (inferior aspect) | Oculomotor n. (CN III), inferior division | Extorts, elevates (esp. when adducted) and abducts the eye
levator palpebrae superioris | Lesser wing of sphenoid, above the optic canal | Skin of upper eyelid and superior tarsal plate | Oculomotor n. (CN III); smooth-muscle part: sympathetic fibres | Elevates the upper eyelid | Ptosis in CN III palsy (complete) or Horner syndrome (partial).

## MASTICATION
masseter | Zygomatic arch (maxillary process of zygomatic bone) | Lateral surface of ramus and angle of mandible | Masseteric n. (CN V3, mandibular division) | Elevates mandible (closes jaw) | Strongest muscle by force for its size; palpable when clenching teeth.
part:superficial part of masseter | Maxillary process of zygomatic bone and anterior 2/3 of zygomatic arch | Angle and lower lateral ramus of mandible
part:deep part of masseter | Posterior 1/3 and medial surface of zygomatic arch | Upper ramus and coronoid process of mandible
temporalis | Temporal fossa and deep surface of temporal fascia | Coronoid process and anterior border of ramus of mandible | Deep temporal nn. (CN V3) | Elevates mandible (anterior fibres); retracts mandible (posterior fibres)
lateral pterygoid | Superior head: infratemporal surface of greater wing of sphenoid. Inferior head: lateral surface of lateral pterygoid plate | Pterygoid fovea of mandibular neck; articular disc and capsule of TMJ | Nerve to lateral pterygoid (CN V3) | Protrudes mandible and depresses chin (opens mouth); alternating contraction gives side-to-side grinding | The only masticatory muscle that opens the mouth.
part:superior head of lateral pterygoid | Infratemporal surface and crest of greater wing of sphenoid | Articular disc and capsule of TMJ | Stabilises the disc during closing
part:inferior head of lateral pterygoid | Lateral surface of lateral pterygoid plate | Pterygoid fovea on neck of mandible | Protrudes and depresses the mandible
medial pterygoid | Deep head: medial surface of lateral pterygoid plate and palatine bone. Superficial head: maxillary tuberosity | Medial surface of mandibular angle (pterygoid tuberosity) | Nerve to medial pterygoid (CN V3) | Elevates and protrudes mandible; side-to-side grinding | Forms a sling with masseter around the angle of the mandible.

part:orbital part of orbicularis oculi | Medial orbital margin and medial palpebral ligament | Circular fibres around orbit, blending with adjacent muscles | Forceful eye closure, "screwing up" the eyes

## TONGUE & PALATE
genioglossus | Superior mental spine of the mandible | Entire dorsum of the tongue and body of hyoid | Hypoglossal n. (CN XII) | Protrudes the tongue (and depresses it) | The "safety muscle": on protrusion the tongue deviates TOWARD the side of a CN XII lesion.
hyoglossus | Body and greater horn of the hyoid | Side of the tongue | Hypoglossal n. (CN XII) | Depresses and retracts the tongue | Lingual artery passes deep to it.
palatopharyngeus | Palatine aponeurosis of the soft palate | Posterior border of thyroid cartilage and side of pharynx | Pharyngeal plexus (CN X) | Elevates the pharynx and larynx; closes the oropharyngeal isthmus (forms the palatopharyngeal arch)

## HYOID MUSCLES

## LARYNX
cricothyroid | Anterolateral surface of the cricoid cartilage | Inferior horn and lower border of the thyroid lamina | External laryngeal n. (branch of superior laryngeal n., CN X) | Tenses the vocal folds (raises pitch) | The only intrinsic laryngeal muscle NOT supplied by the recurrent laryngeal nerve.
part:oblique part of cricothyroid | Anterolateral cricoid | Inferior horn of thyroid cartilage | Tilts the thyroid cartilage forward
part:straight part of cricothyroid | Anterior cricoid | Lower border of the thyroid lamina | Tilts the thyroid cartilage forward
posterior crico-arytenoid | Posterior surface of the cricoid lamina | Muscular process of the arytenoid | Recurrent laryngeal n. (CN X) | Abducts the vocal folds (opens the rima glottidis) | The ONLY abductor of the vocal folds; bilateral paralysis causes stridor and airway obstruction.
lateral crico-arytenoid | Arch of the cricoid cartilage | Muscular process of the arytenoid | Recurrent laryngeal n. (CN X) | Adducts the vocal folds (closes rima glottidis)
transverse arytenoid | Posterior surface of one arytenoid cartilage | Posterior surface of the opposite arytenoid | Recurrent laryngeal n. (CN X) | Adducts the arytenoids; closes the posterior part of the rima glottidis | The only unpaired intrinsic laryngeal muscle.
oblique arytenoid | Posterior surface of muscular process of one arytenoid | Apex of the opposite arytenoid; continues as aryepiglottic muscle | Recurrent laryngeal n. (CN X) | Narrows the laryngeal inlet (sphincter action)
part:ary-epiglottic part of oblique arytenoid | Apex of arytenoid | Lateral border of epiglottis via aryepiglottic fold | Narrows the laryngeal inlet
thyro-arytenoid | Lower half of the thyroid angle and cricothyroid ligament | Anterolateral surface of the arytenoid | Recurrent laryngeal n. (CN X) | Relaxes the vocal folds; narrows the inlet; its medial fibres (vocalis) fine-tune pitch
part:external part of thyro-arytenoid | Thyroid angle | Muscular process and lateral arytenoid | Sphincter of the glottis
part:thyro-epiglottic part of thyro-arytenoid | Thyroid angle | Lateral border of epiglottis | Widens the laryngeal inlet

## PHARYNX
