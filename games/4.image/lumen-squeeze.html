About Lumen Squeeze

What is this app?

Lumen Squeeze is a free image converter and compressor that runs entirely inside your browser. There is no account, no upload and no server involved: your pictures are decoded, resized, re-encoded and saved on your own device.

  • Convert between formats: pick JPG, PNG, WebP or AVIF as the output.
  • Compress to a quality you choose, or to an exact file size such as 100 KB or 2 MB.
  • Resize and crop by longest side, free-form crop, straighten and perspective fix.
  • Edit lightly with filters, frames and captions (passport-style borders, name and date strips).
  • Batch as many images or whole folders as you like, then download one ZIP.
  • More tools: HEIC to JPG in one tap, background remover, favicon generator, social media sizes, images to PDF, PDF to images and bulk rename.
  • Protect privacy: re-encoding removes EXIF data such as GPS location and camera serial numbers.
User manual
  1. Add images. Drop files on the box, tap Choose images, pick a Folder, or paste with Ctrl/⌘+V. Not sure yet? Tap Try a sample.
  2. Choose an output format. Auto picks the smaller of WebP and JPEG for every image. Choose JPEG, WebP, PNG or AVIF to force one format.
  3. Set the size. Use the Quality slider, or set a Target file size (chip or any custom KB value) and the app searches for the best quality that fits. The quality slider is disabled while a target is active.
  4. Resize if needed. Max side limits the longest edge in pixels (4K, 1920, 1280… or a custom number). Smaller dimensions are the biggest single way to cut file size.
  5. Review the result. Each card shows the new size and the percentage saved. The summary bar shows your total savings.
  6. Fine-tune a single image with the buttons on its card:
    • ✂ Crop – crop, rotate, flip, straighten, fix perspective, brightness, contrast and filters.
    • 🪪 Frame – add a border and up to three lines of text.
    • ⚙ Tune – give this image its own quality, format, size or target that overrides the global settings.
    • 🔍 Inspect – drag a before/after slider and see a quality-versus-size curve with a suggested sweet spot.
    • ⬇ Save / ✕ Remove – download or discard that image.
  7. More tools. Scroll to More tools for the favicon generator, social sizes, PDF conversion, bulk rename and the background remover (also available as 🪄 Cutout on each image).
  8. Download. Save images one by one, or use Download all (ZIP). Use Clear to start over.

Quick recipes

  • Smaller photos for email or web: Auto format, quality 70–80, Max side 1920.
  • Meet an upload limit: set a target size, for example 200 KB.
  • Convert HEIC from an iPhone to JPG: tap 📱 HEIC → JPG and pick your photos.
  • Website icons: add a square logo, open Favicon generator and download the ZIP.
  • Post to every network: open Social media sizes, tick the platforms and download.
  • Screenshots, logos and transparency: choose PNG or WebP.

The app can only open what your browser can decode. If a file will not load, try another browser (Safari for HEIC and TIFF) or export it as JPG or PNG first.

Privacy, limits and good to know
  • Nothing is uploaded. Files never leave your device. Closing the tab discards everything.
  • Metadata is removed. EXIF details (GPS, camera model, dates) are not carried into the output.
  • Very large images (over about 16 megapixels) are scaled down while processing to keep your browser responsive.
  • Re-compressing an already compressed JPG loses a little more quality each time. Keep your originals.
  • Lossy vs lossless: JPG, WebP and AVIF trade quality for size. PNG keeps every pixel.
  • Dark mode: use the ◐ button at the top to switch themes.
Frequently asked questions

How do I convert HEIC to JPG?
Add your HEIC photos, set the output format to JPEG, raise quality to 90 or more and download. Tap the HEIC → JPG button. Safari opens HEIC directly and other browsers use a built-in converter that loads on first use.

How do I compress an image to 100 KB?
Pick the 100 KB chip under Target file size, or type any value in KB. The app searches for the best quality that fits under that limit.

Is Lumen Squeeze really free and private?
Yes. There is no sign-up and no upload. Conversion and compression run in your browser, so your images never leave your device.

Will compressing reduce image quality?
WebP, JPEG and AVIF are lossy, but at quality 70–80 the difference is usually hard to see. Use Inspect to compare before and after.

Which format is smallest: JPG, PNG, WebP or AVIF?
For photos AVIF is usually smallest, then WebP, then JPG. PNG is lossless and best for screenshots and logos.

Can I remove a background for free?
Yes. The background remover works on plain backgrounds, keeps transparency as PNG or WebP and can fill the area with a colour. It is colour-based, not AI, so complex scenes may need manual tuning.

How do I make a favicon from an image?
Add a square logo, open the Favicon generator and download the ZIP with favicon.ico, PNG icons, Apple touch icon, manifest and the HTML to paste.

Can I turn images into a PDF, or a PDF into images?
Yes. Use Images → PDF to combine your images, or PDF → images to turn each page into a picture you can compress.

Can I convert many images at once?
Yes. Drop multiple files or a whole folder and download everything as one ZIP.

Everything runs in your browser using the Canvas API. Re-encoding also removes EXIF metadata such as GPS location and camera serial numbers.
