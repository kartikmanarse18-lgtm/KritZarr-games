FaceVibe Pro 🧬

A guided 5-angle face scan for fun. Playful geometry, not a judgment of how you look.

Before we start

Your camera is analyzed on this device only. Nothing is uploaded. Photos are never saved. Only your scores are kept locally, and you can delete them anytime.

Good light and a steady hand help.

Drag or use arrow keys to rotate your 3D face cloud.

Symmetry (fun estimate)
What’s this?Compares matching landmark pairs (eyes, brows, mouth, nose, cheeks) either side of your face’s centerline. Everyone scores below 100, and small differences are normal.
Peak smile
What’s this?The strongest smile the tracker saw in any of your captures.
Expression range (rough)
What’s this?A loose measure of how much your brows, jaw, eyes and smile moved between angles.

Every real face is asymmetric, and that’s part of what makes you recognizable. If looking at scores like these affects how you feel, it’s fine to skip them.
