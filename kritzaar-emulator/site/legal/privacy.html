Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-13

Short version

Your game files and their names stay on your device. We do not upload them and do not log them.

What stays on your device

  • Game files, save states, settings, and "last played" times, stored in your browser's IndexedDB.
  • Offline copies of the site, kept by a service worker.

You can delete games in My Games, or clear site data in your browser at any time.

What we collect

We may use privacy-friendly, cookieless analytics (for example Cloudflare Web Analytics) to count page views. It does not include your game files or file names.

Advertising

Pages with original written content may show ads from Google AdSense, which may use cookies according to Google's policy. There are no ads on the player page. [Review: add consent banner details if required for your audience.]

Third-party links

Catalog pages link to official sources. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Children

KritZaar is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Your choices

Clear your browser's site data to remove everything stored on your device. Use Backup in My Games to keep a copy first.
