Copyright and DMCA Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-13

Our approach

KritZaar hosts no game files. We link only to official sources and publish original descriptions. We respect intellectual property and act quickly on valid notices.

How to send a takedown notice

If you believe something on KritZaar infringes your copyright (for example a catalog entry, a link, an image, or text), email DMCA_EMAIL_HERE with:

  1. Your name, company, and contact details.
  2. The exact KritZaar page URL(s) concerned.
  3. A description of the work you own and where it can be seen.
  4. A statement that you have a good-faith belief the use is not authorized by you, your agent, or the law.
  5. A statement that the information is accurate and, under penalty of perjury, that you are the owner or authorized to act for the owner.
  6. Your physical or electronic signature.

What happens next

  1. Acknowledgment: we reply within 2 business days.
  2. Review: we check the notice and the page.
  3. Action: for a valid notice we set the catalog entry to removed, which takes the game page, search result, and sitemap entry down, and remove any disputed image or text.
  4. Confirmation: we tell you what was done.

Counter-notice

If you believe content was removed by mistake, send a counter-notice to the same address with the page URL, your contact details, a statement under penalty of perjury that you believe the removal was a mistake, and your signature. We may restore the content as the law allows.

Repeat infringement

We may stop listing sources or content tied to repeated, valid complaints.

Good-faith note

Knowingly false notices may have legal consequences. This page is general information, not legal advice.
