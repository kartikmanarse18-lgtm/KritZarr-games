PhonePad

Use an Android phone as the mouse, keyboard, media remote, webcam and microphone of an old PC that has none of them.

Download for Android

Download APK
Android 5.0+ (Bluetooth mouse/keyboard needs Android 9+). Allow “install unknown apps” when asked.
Updating? Uninstall the old version first, then install the new APK.

PC receiver (backup “Net” mode)

Works on any phone. Needs only Python 3 on the Windows PC.

run_receiver.bat phonepad_receiver.py

1. Mouse & keyboard (Bluetooth)

  1. In the app tap Visible, then pair the phone from the PC’s Bluetooth settings.
  2. Tap Connect.

Tap = click · two-finger tap = right click · double-tap-and-drag = drag · two-finger swipe = scroll. Full US keyboard, Ctrl/Shift/Alt/Win, F1–F12, arrows and media keys.

2. Camera & microphone for the PC

Tap Stream, choose camera/mic, then Start streaming. The phone serves:

URLContent
http://PHONE_IP:8080/Live video + audio page
/videoMJPEG video
/snapshot.jpgSingle frame
/audioWAV stream (16 kHz mono)

The PC must reach the phone: USB tethering, phone hotspot, or the same router.
